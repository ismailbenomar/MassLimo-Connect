## Direction contract

THESIS: MassLimo Connect presents transportation referrals through the visual language of contemporary airport wayfinding and mobility editorial. It refuses the expected black-and-gold limousine brochure and the generic card-heavy software landing page.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white fields, near-black type, decisive cobalt wayfinding, full-bleed automotive photography, square controls, crisp rules, compact route codes, and high-contrast shifts between bright editorial space and dark operational sections.

STORY: A visitor immediately understands that the service connects Massachusetts travelers with independent limousine and chauffeur providers, chooses a trip type, learns the three referral steps, checks common regions, and calls or requests a callback.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A cinematic sedan fills the screen. A large left-aligned headline, plain explanation, and two actions sit over the darkened image; the confirmed phone action leads. A cobalt service ticker closes the viewport and introduces the site’s wayfinding language.

FORM: Contemporary airport departure-board editorial, fourth in the grounded direction list. FORM seed key: f704b664. The screen uses route codes, precise horizontal rules, direct labels, and a single cobalt signal color without imitating a literal flight board.
