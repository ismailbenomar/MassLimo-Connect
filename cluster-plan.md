# MassLimo Connect Topic Cluster Plan

**Seed topic:** Finding and evaluating transportation providers in Massachusetts  
**Created:** September 26, 2026  
**Architecture:** One informational pillar, four clusters, twelve spokes, five existing commercial service pages

## Strategic decision

Search results for “Boston Logan airport car service,” corporate transportation, wedding transportation, hourly chauffeur service, and group transportation are dominated by direct operators. MassLimo Connect is a referral service, so publishing operator-style pages about owned fleets, prices, availability, or service delivery would create an intent and trust mismatch.

The content cluster should own a different position: helping Massachusetts travelers prepare a request, evaluate an independent provider, and understand the referral process. Existing service pages remain the commercial landing pages. New guides support informational and commercial-research intent and send qualified readers to the relevant referral page.

No paid volume or keyword-difficulty data was available. Priority is based on observed SERP formats, relevance to the existing service pages, conversion proximity, and the gaps identified in the SEO/GEO audits.

## Internal-link architecture

Every spoke must:

1. Link to the pillar with a descriptive variant of “choosing a Massachusetts transportation provider.”
2. Link to both sibling spokes in its cluster.
3. Link to its relevant existing service or referral page.
4. Receive links from the pillar and both siblings, giving it at least three incoming contextual links.

The pillar links to all twelve spokes in their relevant sections. Existing service pages should link back to the most useful guide rather than adding a generic blog feed. Cross-cluster links should be limited to clear traveler journeys, such as the wedding checklist linking to group vehicle planning or the corporate checklist linking to Logan preparation.

## Publishing order

1. Pillar: choosing a transportation provider in Massachusetts.
2. How transportation referrals work.
3. Questions to ask a provider.
4. Logan Airport request checklist.
5. Boston wedding transportation checklist.
6. Corporate provider checklist.
7. Hourly vs point-to-point comparison.

Publish the pillar and the first two provider-selection spokes together so the new section launches as a connected resource rather than a thin standalone article.

## Cannibalization safeguards

- Keep direct commercial terms on the existing service pages.
- Do not create separate posts for synonymous variants such as “Logan request information” and “what details are needed for Logan car service.”
- Keep “questions to ask a provider” broad; trip-specific checklists should address only their distinct use case.
- Avoid city pages that repeat the same guide with a substituted location.
- Each guide needs a unique primary intent, factual sources, and a visible route to the appropriate referral page.

## Measurement

After the production domain launches, use Search Console and Bing data to validate impressions, query overlap, and cannibalization. Paid keyword volumes are optional; actual query and conversion data should determine later publishing priority.
